Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: email raj@rivesa.ai. We reply within two working days.

The phone doesn't ring

On iOS 26 or later, allow alarms for Towardly in Settings → Towardly. On earlier versions, allow notifications and Time Sensitive notifications. Check Profile → Check-ins for your waking hours and interval.

Change how often it asks

Tap the interval under the ring on Today, or open Profile → Check-ins. If today has nothing recorded yet the change applies now; otherwise it starts tomorrow.

Moving to a new iPhone

Sign in with Apple on both phones. While you're signed in, a private copy of your data is kept with your account and restored on the new phone.

Delete your account and data

Open Profile → Account → Delete account and confirm with Apple. This erases your data on the iPhone and the copy kept with your account, and deletes the account. Without an account, Profile → Account → Erase all data removes everything from the phone. You can also email raj@rivesa.ai to ask for deletion.
