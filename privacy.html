Privacy policy

Summary

Closer keeps your goal, your check-ins and your settings on this iPhone. An account is optional. Without one, nothing leaves the phone. If you continue with Apple, an account identifier and email address sign you in, and a private copy of your app data is kept with your account so a reinstall or a new iPhone can restore it. No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and your data is never sold.

Information in the app

Your name (if you give one), your goal and why it matters, your plan (cycle length, check-in interval, wake time and sleep hours), your appearance and sound settings, and the Toward / Against answer for each block are stored in a file inside the app on your device. A photo you choose for your avatar stays on this iPhone and is never uploaded.

Optional Sign in with Apple

If you choose Continue with Apple, Apple provides an account identifier and your email address, which may be an Apple private relay address. Supabase, our authentication and storage provider, uses them to create and sign in to your Closer account. Session credentials are stored in your device's Keychain, and Closer never sees your Apple password.

Your cloud copy

While you are signed in, a copy of the information in the app (your name if you gave one, your goal, why, plan, settings and check-in history) is stored in Supabase. Only your account can read it. It is used to restore your data and for nothing else. Signing out stops the copy from updating; deleting your account deletes it.

Your account

Sign out ends this device's sign-in session and keeps your data on the phone; it does not delete your account. Delete account (Erase all data, without an account) permanently erases your profile and history on this iPhone and in your cloud copy, and cancels every scheduled alarm and notification. If you signed in with Apple, Closer then deletes your account, revokes its sessions and, where available, Apple's authorization. If the same Apple account is still used by another Rivesa app, only Closer's link to it is removed. Deletion can't be undone.

Alarms and notifications

If you allow it, Closer schedules local notifications and, on iOS 26 or later, AlarmKit alarms so it can ring at the end of each block. They are scheduled on the device by iOS; nothing is sent to the developer. You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings, or turn "Ring like a timer" off in Profile.

What we don't collect

No location, contacts, camera, microphone, health data, advertising identifier, device fingerprint, crash analytics or usage analytics. Closer contains no third-party SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Storage and backups

Device backups (iCloud or computer) may include the app's data according to your iOS settings; those backups are managed by you. Deleting the app removes its on-device data but not an account, so use Delete account for that.

Children

Closer is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Support

If you email support, Rivesa Inc. receives your email address and anything you include, and uses it only to reply. Please don't include sensitive information.

Contact and changes

Questions or requests: raj@rivesa.ai. This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change; the date below changes when it does.
