Towardly

The only app built to make you successful.

Set one goal. Every 20 minutes of your waking day, your iPhone rings like a timer and asks one question: did that block move you toward your goal, or against it? One tap answers it. Answers stack into the day, days become a green and red journal, and ninety days of honest answers show you exactly where your success is coming from.

One goal. Write it once, with why it matters.
One question, every block. Every 20 minutes to 4 hours, your choice. Rings through silent mode on iOS 26.
Honest receipts. Every day, week and 90-day cycle prints a receipt of toward and against.

No ads, no tracking, and an account is optional. Towardly is made by Rivesa Inc.
